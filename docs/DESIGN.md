# Design

## Direction

Quiet, technical, and trustworthy. Dark green-black surfaces with a high-visibility lime accent communicate an analytical internal tool without imitating YouTube or Google branding.

## Tokens

- Background: `#0a0d0c`
- Surface: `#111714`
- Text: `#f1f7f3`
- Muted: `#9dafaa`
- Accent: `#c7ff42`
- Maximum width: `1120px`
- Body measure: approximately `67ch`
